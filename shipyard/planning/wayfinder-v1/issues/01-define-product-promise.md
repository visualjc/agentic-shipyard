# Define Shipyard's product promise

Type: grilling  
Status: resolved

## Question

What problem is Shipyard solving, and which audience should anchor its product
positioning?

## Answer

Shipyard lets agentic workflows retain the plans, state, graphs, context,
acceptance evidence, and review records they need without forcing that
scaffolding into a destination repository whose maintainers do not want it.

Mixed-adoption teams and contained metadata are the primary differentiator.
AI-native teams may instead share selected records. Shipyard never hides product
changes or bypasses destination governance: reviewed product changes still
arrive through the destination's normal pull-request and human-merge process.

The product name is **Shipyard**. Its concise promise is: "Shipyard keeps the
scaffolding in the yard and launches only the reviewed cargo."
