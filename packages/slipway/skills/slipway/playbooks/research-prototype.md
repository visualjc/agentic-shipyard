# Research and prototype

Apply the [run start contract](../references/run-start.md) before research or prototype work. Treat branch/shard creation as autonomous local initialization, not a research approval gate.

## Research

Invoke `research` for a focused factual question. Read-only research may proceed without another gate. Require primary sources, a cited Markdown report on the agentic work branch, and the decision it unblocks. Index the canonical report without copying it into the ledger.

## Prototype

Invoke `prototype` for one empirical design question. Keep code throwaway and isolated from delivery cargo. Record the question, branch or artifact, runnable command, observations, and disposition. Always stop for human feedback before turning the result into a product decision. Production keeps the adopted decision, not prototype code.

Return to the originating small or large playbook after the decision is settled.
